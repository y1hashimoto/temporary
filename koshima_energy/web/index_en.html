OUR BUSINESS

Keeping the region's energy flowing — without interruption.

Koshima Energy operates district heating & cooling across two supply areas, delivering chilled water, hot water and steam from central plants. Our customers include hospitals and data centers — facilities that cannot go a single day without supply.

🔥

District heating & steam

Reliable heating, hot water and steam even in the depths of winter — centrally operated, so each building needs no heat source of its own.

❄️

District cooling & chilled water

Central chilled-water plants that carry peak summer cooling demand, meeting high-reliability needs such as data-center cooling.

🏥

Stable supply to critical sites

For hospitals and data centers, an outage threatens lives and social functions. Redundancy and 24-hour monitoring protect our "never stop" promise.

🌱

Energy saving & decarbonization

Area-wide thermal use maximizes efficiency and helps cut CO₂ across the whole district.
